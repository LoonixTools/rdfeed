## v1.0.0

_2026-10-02_

Welcome to the very first release of rdfeed! It brings the RemoteApps of your company to Linux: add the workspace once, and Word, Excel and the other apps show up in your app menu and open as windows of their own.

### Highlights

- The apps of your company in the app menu
- Open your files in them with "Open with"
- Signs in like Windows, also with a work e-mail address
- Works with MFA on the gateway
- Sharp on scaled screens, with the right keyboard layout
- New apps show up by themselves every day
- The password stays in the keyring
- In 14 languages
